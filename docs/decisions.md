## 2026-10-06: Step 3.5 financial statements and year close
- **Year close does not carry balances forward as khata entries (deviation from the step prompt; owner to confirm).** The prompt says "carry forward closing balances as opening balances of the new FY (as opening_balance entries + journal)". The khata is cumulative, so an opening udhaar on 1 April alone would double every balance; posting a closing jama on 31 March as well would net out but restart grace tranches and change interest (money logic). So closing posts ONE journal entry dated 31 March (`source_type = year_close`) that zeroes every income and expense account into Profit & Loss A/c and Opening Balance Equity into Capital; the next year's opening balances are simply the closing balances (statements compute them). Recorded in posting-rules 11.5.
- "Post interest (optional run)" is a link to the existing bulk interest posting as of 1 April, not a separate engine run; closing needs the books checks to pass and earlier years closed (in order); re-opening is not offered in v1 (server refuses).

The reason is stored on the journal entry (`lock_reason`, new column) and in its audit row. The server refuses khata entries, journal entries, payments, vouchers, expenses and book lines dated in a closed year unless the uploader is an owner, and a journal entry there without a reason (trigger `guard_locked_period`). Lots, loans, interest postings and cash counts are not listed separately because every one of them writes a khata or journal entry that the trigger checks.
